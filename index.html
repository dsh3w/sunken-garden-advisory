Real M&A analysis, built by LMU students.

Sunken Garden Advisory is a student-run M&A practice group at Loyola Marymount University, where students analyze real deals in consumer & retail, entertainment and healthcare technology over 8-week cycles and present their findings to LMU finance faculty, investment bankers and other finance professionals.
